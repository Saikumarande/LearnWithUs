Privacy and safety

Your choices stay clear.

LearnWithUs is designed to work without public profiles. Children should use audio and quizzes with a parent, guardian or teacher.

Learning progress on this device

Bookmarks, recently viewed pages, preferences and quiz scores are saved in your browser using local storage. They are not uploaded to a LearnWithUs account. Clearing browser data also removes them.

View or clear local progress →

Anonymous analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow anonymous analytics.” It helps count visits and understand useful pages. LearnWithUs does not intentionally send names, email addresses, quiz answers or voice recordings.

Audio

Letter, number and animal audio uses the browser’s speech feature. Available voices depend on the browser and device. LearnWithUs does not record or store a child’s voice.

Health and nutrition

Health and nutrition pages are educational. They do not diagnose symptoms or provide personalised medical, allergy, weight-loss or dietary treatment. Contact a qualified professional for individual advice, and emergency services for urgent problems.
