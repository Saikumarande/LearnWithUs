Time & Calendar

Read clocks and understand days, weeks and months.

Understand the idea first

Time tells us when things happen and how long they take; calendars organise days, weeks, months and years.

Read a clock

The short hand shows the hour. The long hand shows minutes.

Minute landmarks

At 12 it is :00, at 3 it is :15, at 6 it is :30, and at 9 it is :45.

Calendar order

A week has 7 days and a year has 12 months. Learn their order so you can work out yesterday, tomorrow and dates.

Example

3:30 means half past 3 because 30 minutes of the hour have passed.
