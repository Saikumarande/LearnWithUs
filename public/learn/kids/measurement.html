Length, Weight & Capacity

Compare and measure length, weight and capacity.

Understand the idea first

Measurement helps us compare how long, heavy or full something is by using standard units.

Length

Use centimetres for smaller lengths and metres for larger everyday lengths. 100 cm = 1 m.

Weight / mass

Use grams for light objects and kilograms for heavier objects. 1,000 g = 1 kg.

Capacity

Use millilitres for small amounts of liquid and litres for larger amounts. 1,000 mL = 1 L.

Choose the sensible unit

A pencil is measured in centimetres, a person in kilograms, and a bottle may hold litres or millilitres.
