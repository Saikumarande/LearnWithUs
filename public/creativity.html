🎨 Make · move · imagine

Creativity Studio

Draw, colour, connect dots, make crafts, follow a rhythm, move your body, build a story and create your own poem. Everything is arranged one activity at a time so it is easy to follow.

✏️ Drawing lessons

Choose one of 20 subjects, follow the steps, see a simple reference, then draw it yourself. When you are ready, check your drawing for an effort score.

Reference picture

    Draw a picture, then choose Check my drawing.

    🖍️ Colouring page

    Pick one of 30 pictures from the dropdown, choose from 20 colours, then tap parts of the picture to paint them.

    🔢 Join-the-dots

    Select a picture, then tap the numbered dots in order to reveal that shape.

    ✂️ Paper crafts

    Choose one of 10 simple crafts and follow the picture-supported steps with adult help.

      📄 Origami

      Choose one of 10 paper-folding ideas. The simple step cards below help children follow along.

        🥁 Music & rhythm

        Listen to four beats, then tap four beats back. Pick an instrument sound to make the activity more fun.

        Your taps: 0 / 4

        💃 Dance movement

        Choose or randomise a movement. Use the pose picture to copy the action safely in your space.

        🪄 Story creation

        Use the dropdowns to make a longer story starter. Every dropdown now has 10 options.

        📝 Build-your-own poem

        Choose words from dropdowns and build a short four-line poem. Each dropdown has 10 options.

        🖨️ Printable activity sheet

        Prepare your own creativity sheet, print it and share your idea with family or friends.
