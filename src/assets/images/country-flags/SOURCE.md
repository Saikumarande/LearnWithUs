Flag raster illustrations made from country flag glyphs in Noto Color Emoji, for the 48 countries already present in LearnWithUs World Data. Flag illustrations are not official government image assets and are stylized waving representations. Original Noto Color Emoji project: https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji.
